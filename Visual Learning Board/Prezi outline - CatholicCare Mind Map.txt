Speaker notes
Men’s Behaviour Change (MBC): MBC works with men using violence, keeping the safety of women and children at the centre. We run in Rockhampton twice a week and in Emerald fortnightly.
Counselling: The Counselling team provides therapeutic interventions and takes internal referrals from our other programs.
Child Witness: Child Witness supports children who have seen or lived through domestic and family violence, keeping their voices and their safety at the centre.
Supporting Adolescent Boys (SAB): SAB is early intervention. We work with adolescent boys and their families on behaviour change pathways before patterns set in.
Where the overlap occurs: The programs don’t work in silos. Families, risk and referrals cross between them, and this overlap is where my role adds the most value.
