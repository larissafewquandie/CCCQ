If something doesn’t look right

A button isn’t where these steps say
Prezi moves its menus between versions. Look for the same word (Topic, Background, Notes, Path) in the toolbar or the left panel.
The topic frames hide the picture
Select the topic, open its style options and pick a transparent or invisible frame. The picture then shows through and Prezi still zooms to it.
The picture is blurry when it zooms in
Use the PNG files from this pack. They’re saved at double size so they stay sharp when Prezi zooms.
A topic feels crowded
Keep it to three subtopics. Move anything extra into your speaker notes.
I lost my place in the steps
Scroll to the first unticked box. That’s your next step.
