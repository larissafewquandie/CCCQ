Turn the picture into your presentation

  1. Background. Upload the ecosystem picture as the Prezi background. Use the labelled version for presenting and the blank one to rehearse.
  2. Topics. Put a topic over each of the ten parts, named exactly as on the picture.
  3. Path. Order the path 1 to 10: you, the four programs, the overlap, HRT, then outward to leadership, people and stakeholders. End by zooming back out to the whole picture.
  4. Subtopics. Inside the centre circle, add the nine responsibilities as three groups of three: Leading practice, Leading people, Leading outward. Three groups are easier to hold than nine items.
  5. Speaker notes. Paste the presenter line for each stop into that topic’s notes.
  6. Rehearse with recall mode. Turn on Recall mode here, click each part and say your line before revealing it.
