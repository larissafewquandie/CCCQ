# Cross-Reference: SWFE Learning Agreement 2026 vs CQU Field Education Manual (June 2024)

Prepared for meeting with Robyn Kemble (CQU Field Education Academic Lead), 5 August 2026.

## 1. Confirmed: Robyn Kemble is the right contact
"Welcome to FE2 2025 – Supervisors" names **Robyn Kemble, Field Education Academic Lead**
(r.kemble@cqu.edu.au) as the query contact for FE2, alongside Jacqueline McNamara, Field
Education Coordinator. Matches the Learning Agreement's own metadata (last modified by
Robyn Kemble, 17 July 2026).

## 2. Action item: a 2025 edition of the Manual exists — we only have 2024
The "Welcome to FE2 2025" doc tells supervisors to "review the CQUni Field Education Manual
**2025**." The manual in this pack is the **June 2024 edition**. Ask Robyn for the current
edition (2025, or 2026 if one exists) so the Learning Agreement can be checked against the
version actually in force this term.

## 3. Assessment numbering appears inconsistent between the two documents
The June 2024 Manual's "Summary of Assessment Requirements" defines **three separate
assessments** for SOWK13009/SOWK14009:
- **Assessment 1 – The Learning Journey**: the Learning Agreement itself, submitted in three
  parts (Creating Goals / MPR / EPR) at three points in the placement.
- **Assessment 2 – The Reflective Journey**: weekly supervision/reflection records + peer Moodle
  forum posts.
- **Assessment 3 – Social Work Practice Framework (PF)**: a presentation, resubmitted as video
  for final placement.

The 2026 Learning Agreement instead labels its own three internal touchpoints **"Assessment 1 –
Learning Goals," "Assessment 2 – Mid-Placement Review," "Assessment 3 – End of Placement
Review"** — i.e. it reuses "Assessment 1/2/3" for the three parts of what the manual calls just
"Assessment 1."

**Ask Robyn directly:** has the unit's overall assessment structure changed (Reflective Journey /
Practice Framework dropped or renumbered), or is this a naming inconsistency between the form
and the current Unit/Assessment Outlines that should be tidied up so students and supervisors
aren't confused about how many assessments there are?

## 4. Inherent Requirements document is a stale snapshot
"4. CQU Social Work – Inherent Requirements CQUni Handbook.pdf" is a browser print-out of
`handbook.cqu.edu.au/he/courses/view/CL71`, timestamped **23 June 2022** — over four years
old, predating even the 2024 manual. Recommend pulling a fresh copy from the live handbook page
before treating it as current guidance for reasonable-adjustment or fitness-to-practice discussions.

## 5. ASWEAS 2021 references are now outdated
The June 2024 manual cites **ASWEAS (2021)** throughout (hours requirements, framework for
assessment, reasonable-adjustment sections). ASWEAS was updated to **ASWEAS 2024**
(November 2024). Worth checking whether the 2025 manual (see #2) has been updated to cite
ASWEAS 2024, since the accreditation basis itself has moved.

## 6. Good news: the Learning Agreement's standards guide is a faithful extract
Appendix A of the Learning Agreement ("Practice Standards Guide") reproduces the manual's
"Framework for Assessment" 1–9 list almost verbatim (e.g. "Social workers conduct themselves
according to the values, principles and guidelines of the AASW Code of Ethics 2020" appears
word-for-word in both). The four-Practice-Area grouping (Standards 1+3 / 2+4 / 5+6 / 7+8+9) is a
CQU-specific structuring on top of the AASW's 9 standards — internally consistent, not an
error.

## 7. Self-care placeholder is intentional; Professional Communication is plausible but unconfirmed
The pre-filled goal (c) rows — "Self-Care" under Practice Area 4 — map directly onto Learning
Outcome 2 for both SOWK13009 and SOWK14009 ("self-care strategies... use of self"), so that's
confirmed as deliberate, not leftover drafting text. "Professional Communication" under Practice
Area 3(c) isn't explicitly named anywhere in the 2024 manual — worth a quick confirmation with
Robyn that it's an intended fixed prompt rather than something left in from drafting.

## 8. Supervision hours not stated on the form
"Welcome to FE2 2025" specifies **1.5 hours/week** of supervision as the supervisor's minimum
commitment. The Learning Agreement doesn't state this figure anywhere. Worth asking whether it
should be, given the form is the document supervisors and students refer back to.

## 9. SONIA / portal access — practical, not just paperwork
The source email thread ("SONIA Access and CQU Portal Enquiry") lines up with real dependencies
in the FE2 process: supervisors sign off the Log of Hours and complete the End-of-Placement report
through SONIA-generated links. If access was the original issue, it's worth confirming today that
Brea's supervisor(s) have working SONIA access before the Mid-Placement Review window opens —
paperwork can't proceed without it.

## 10. Supplementary reading — not CQU documents, no currency concern
"A guide to supervision in social work field education" (CSU/ALTC, Revised Ed., Nov 2010) and "How
to grow a social worker" (UNSW) are general good-practice booklets, not CQU policy documents.
Their age doesn't matter in the same way — they're background reading, not something that needs
to match the current unit outline.
