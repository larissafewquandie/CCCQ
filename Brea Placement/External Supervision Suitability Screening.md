# External Supervision Suitability Screening – Social Work Student Placements

Prepared for CatholicCare CQ, for use when a staff member is proposed as the agency supervisor
for a CQU BSW student (currently Brea's FE2 placement). It sets out what each source document in
this folder says about who may supervise, when an external social work supervisor is needed, and
what each supervisor must do, so you can check your staff against it.

**Scope:** This uses only the documents in `Brea Placement/`. The folder has no data on our staff,
such as their qualifications, AASW membership, years of practice, workloads or supervision
records. Part 2 is a test you fill in for each staff member. It is not a completed assessment.

## Part 1 – The rules, with sources

### 1.1 Who can be the Field Educator (on-site social work supervisor)

| Requirement | What the source says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified social worker | Field Educator "is a social worker" and is the student's direct contact in the placement organisation. | CQU Field Ed Manual 2024, p.15 |
| At least 2 years post-qualifying practice | Role "performed by a social worker with at least 2 years post-graduate practice in the field." | CQU Manual 2024, p.15; also UNSW *How to Grow a Social Worker*, p.8 ("you must have been in the field for two years before you can take a student", citing AASW) |
| Qualification must be recognised by the AASW | External supervision "usually occurs when the on-site supervisor's training is not recognised as social work training by the AASW (e.g. a non-social work degree or an overseas social work degree)." A recognised qualification is a BSW or MSW (Qualifying). | UNSW *How to Grow*, Glossary pp.6–7 |
| A social worker with under 2 years' experience counts as a task supervisor, not a field educator | Task supervisor is "an experienced human services worker who isn't social work qualified **or a new social worker with less than two years' experience since graduation**." | CSU/ALTC *Guide to Supervision in SW Field Education* (2010), p.34 |
| Supervisor must be appropriately trained and senior | Formal supervision "by an appropriately trained, senior social work qualified supervisor." | AASW Practice Standards 2023, Standard 8.1, p.14 |

### 1.2 When an external supervisor is needed, and who arranges it

- **Trigger:** "In cases where a social worker is not available on site for this role, **CQUni will
  appoint** a social worker external to the agency, to provide the 1 – 1.5 hours of formal social work
  supervision required." (CQU Manual 2024, p.16)
- The non-social-work staff member then becomes the **Onsite/Task Supervisor**. That person
  "introduce[s] you to the organisation, oversee[s] your day-to-day practice, offer[s] feedback and task
  supervision, and support[s] your learning agreement goals in conjunction with the external
  supervisor." (CQU Manual 2024, p.16)
- **CatholicCare CQ does not have to find the external supervisor.** Under the manual, CQU
  appoints them. Our task is to tell CQU early that we have no eligible on-site social worker.

### 1.3 ASWEAS rules that can block an external supervision arrangement

From the "Key ASWEAS points" in the CQU Manual 2024, p.9:

- **4.1.6:** "Students will complete **at least one placement** in a professional practice setting
  **with an onsite social work field educator**." If Brea's FE1 placement used external supervision,
  her FE2 placement at CCCQ **must** have an on-site social work Field Educator. External supervision
  will not meet this rule. Check this first.
- **4.1.7:** "Students in employment will not have a placement in their current work role or under
  their current supervisor." If Brea is also a CCCQ employee, her placement supervisor cannot be her
  line manager, and her placement cannot be in her current role.

### 1.4 How much supervision is required

| Source | Requirement |
|---|---|
| CQU Manual 2024, roles table p.16 | "At least **1.5 hours** of formal structured supervision per **35 hours** of placement" |
| CQU Manual 2024, p.15 (Field Educator definition) | "1 – 1.5 hours per 5 days of attendance" |
| CQU Manual 2024, p.16 (External Supervisor definition) | "1 – 1.5 hours of formal social work supervision" |
| *Welcome to FE2 2025 – Supervisors* | "Provide **1.5 hours** of supervision per week" |
| CQU Manual 2024, p.31 | Student must have "regular reflective supervision with a qualified social worker" |
| UNSW *How to Grow*, p.34 | Individual supervision for every student, "at least on a fortnightly basis" if possible |

⚠ **The documents disagree.** The manual gives "1 – 1.5 hrs" in two places and "at least 1.5 hrs"
in its table. The FE2 2025 welcome letter says 1.5 hrs. **Use 1.5 hrs per 35 hrs of placement (per
week, full-time) as the minimum.** With external supervision, this formal social work supervision
comes from the **external** supervisor. The task supervisor's day-to-day oversight does not count
towards it.

### 1.5 Who does what (CQU Manual 2024, roles table pp.16–17)

| Responsibility | Field Educator | Onsite Task Supervisor | External Supervisor |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Know and follow AASW field education standards | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Help student meet SOWK13009/14009 assessment standards | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Provide quality learning environment and equipment | ✓ | ✓ | |
| **Provide ≥1.5 hrs formal structured supervision per 35 hrs** | ✓ | | ✓ |
| Liaise with external supervisor; supervise operational matters | | ✓ | |
| Raise performance concerns against learning agreement on time | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Raise student conflict concerns on time | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Seek early-intervention consults with Field Ed Team | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Attend Learning Agreement meeting and Mid-Placement Review | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Written end-of-placement evaluation | ✓ | | ✓ |

Other duties:
- **SONIA:** The onsite supervisor signs off the Log of Hours, which also records supervision
  sessions. The professional supervisor completes the End of Placement report through a SONIA link.
  (CQU Manual 2024, pp.17, 31; *Welcome to FE2 2025*)
- **Learning Agreement 2026:** The form has separate fields for "Agency Supervisor" (with
  *Profession*) and "External Social Work Supervisor (if applicable)". Both sign off at each point:
  Learning Goals, MPR and EPR.
- **Fitness to practice:** The external field educator can raise concerns. Their feedback is one of
  the inputs to a formal review. (CQU Manual 2024, pp.23, 27–28)

### 1.6 Making external supervision work (good practice, not mandatory)

From the CSU/ALTC *Guide to Supervision*, p.34:
- An off-site supervisor does not easily see the agency's culture, its day-to-day events or the
  student's practice.
- Hold **2–3 joint supervision sessions** (external supervisor + task supervisor + student) early in
  the placement.
- **All parties attend all university liaison contacts.**
- **Agree who does what early and put it in a written supervision agreement.**
- Anyone can contact the university liaison visitor if co-supervision is not working.

AASW Standard 8 (p.14) also asks for:
- A **record of supervision** kept for the length of the supervisory relationship (8.2).
- Supervision that is **"separate and distinct from line management"** (8.3).
- A balance of education, support and accountability (8.4).

## Part 3 – Gaps and questions for CQU (Robyn Kemble / Jacqueline McNamara)

1. **Which hours figure applies?** The manual gives "1–1.5 hrs" in two places and "≥1.5 hrs" in its
   table. Ask CQU to confirm 1.5 hrs/week and whether the 2025/26 manual has changed it.
2. **Current manual edition.** We only have the June 2024 manual, which cites ASWEAS 2021. Ask for
   the 2025/26 edition. ASWEAS 2024 may have changed the 4.1.6/4.1.7 wording or the supervisor
   eligibility rules. (Also noted in the cross-reference notes.)
3. **Brea's FE1 arrangement.** Confirm whether FE1 had an on-site social work field educator. This
   decides whether external supervision is allowed for FE2 at all.
4. **Is Brea a CCCQ employee?** If she is, ASWEAS 4.1.7 applies.
5. **Who pays the external supervisor, and how are they briefed?** The manual says CQU appoints
   them but does not say who pays or what induction to CCCQ they get.
6. **Ongoing staff supervision.** None of these documents covers supervision for qualified CCCQ
   staff. The only relevant rule is AASW Standard 8 (regular, structured supervision from a senior
   social work qualified supervisor, separate from line management). To check staff supervision
   generally, we would need our supervision register, staff qualifications list and supervision
   policy. None of these is in this repository.
