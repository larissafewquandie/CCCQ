# Team Touchpoint Meetings

Plans and prepares line management touchpoints so Larissa can get back up to speed with each staff member and restore regular structure. Each meeting is 30 minutes unless Larissa says otherwise.

## Inputs to collect

1. Staff names, with the track for each (see Tracks). If the track is unclear, ask. Do not guess probation status.
2. Availability: dates and times. If Larissa pastes availability, build the schedule from it.
3. Any known issues per person: open cases, overdue training, leave, concerns.

## Tracks

| Track | Who it fits | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| A. Structured line management | Ongoing staff who need regular structure restored | Caseload, risk, reporting, training, role changes, set a recurring cadence |
| B. Return from leave | Staff back from extended leave | Wellbeing, what changed while away, platform access, caseload handover, phased return |
| C. Probation | Staff within their probation period | Progress against probation goals, support needed, review dates, formal sign-off |
| D. Student placement | Social work students finishing university (CQU field education) | Learning Agreement goals, supervision hours, MPR/EPR dates, liaison with CQU |

One person can sit on two tracks (for example B and A). Combine the agendas but keep the meeting to 30 minutes. Move anything that doesn't fit into the follow-up list.

## Core agenda (30 minutes)

Every touchpoint uses the same six blocks so records are consistent across the team. Change the questions to fit the track.

1. **Check-in and wellbeing (5 min).** How are they going personally and professionally? Workload pressure, vicarious trauma, EAP reminder.
2. **Caseload and client supports (8 min).** Open cases, high-risk clients (HRT referrals, safety plans), anything stuck, RFIs and collateral checks outstanding, case notes up to date.
3. **HR and compliance (4 min).** Employment Hero records, leave balances, Blue Card and Yellow Card, police check, timesheets. Check in Employment Hero before the meeting. Never assume status.
4. **Training and development (4 min).** Mandatory training overdue or due (check Go1 beforehand), PD interests, practice framework.
5. **Role changes and systems (4 min).** What has changed in the role, which platforms information now goes to, reporting requirements, team meeting schedule.
6. **Agreements and next steps (5 min).** Set the recurring line management cadence. Confirm actions, owners and dates. For Track C, confirm the next probation review date. For Track D, confirm the next supervision date.

## Track-specific questions

- **A:** What would help you most from me right now? What is one thing slowing you down? Agree on fortnightly or monthly line management.
- **B:** What do you need to feel settled back in? Are your logins and platform access working? Which cases have come back to you, and are the handover notes complete? Would a phased caseload help?
- **C:** Note the probation start and end dates. Review progress against the position description and probation goals. Discuss strengths, development areas and support needed. Explain clearly what a successful completion looks like. Record any concerns in writing on the day.
- **D:** Check the Learning Agreement goals and practice areas, supervision hours logged against required hours, Reflective Journey records, and the next CQU assessment point (Mid-Placement or End of Placement Review). Ask whether the liaison visit is booked. Use the materials in `Brea Placement/` for reference.

## Outputs

For each person, produce the following.

1. **Invite email.** Warm and brief, with the date and time. Explain the purpose: Larissa is back from leave and wants to reconnect. Give 2 to 3 things to bring or think about. Use Australian spelling and sign off as Larissa.
2. **Agenda.** The six core blocks with track-specific prompts and the known issues for that person.
3. **Follow-up checklist.** Items Larissa must check before or after the meeting (Employment Hero for HR, leave and probation dates; Go1 for training completions; case management system; CQU).

Then produce a **schedule table** for the whole team and a **list of people still to book**.

## After the meeting

- If the meeting was recorded or Larissa took notes, use the `line-management-summariser` skill to complete the HR 029 Census Line Management Record.
- Use the `meeting-task-extractor` skill to turn the notes into dated actions for Planner or Outlook.

## Style rules

- Australian spelling. Plain, supportive and professional tone.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Never invent compliance status, leave balances, probation dates or case details. Write "Check in Employment Hero" or "Confirm with [name]" instead.
- Don't send emails or create calendar invites without Larissa's explicit go-ahead.
